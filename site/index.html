Working from home blurs the day

At the office the day has edges. At home, a quick look at chat becomes half an hour, and the evening slides into "just one more thing". HomeFocus gives you an honest picture of your day, without judgement and without effort: it just runs in the tray.

What you get

Everything you need to see your working day clearly, nothing you have to babysit.

Automatic tracking

Records the app in front of you once a second. Idle time, breaks and sleep are detected and left out.

Websites too

With the HomeFocus extension for Chrome or Edge, browser time is split per website, so YouTube no longer hides inside "Chrome".

Day timeline

See your day as a colored strip: work, communication, entertainment and the breaks in between.

Weekly & monthly overview

Totals, daily averages, your busiest day and an app ranking. Export everything to CSV.

Your categories

Apps are sorted automatically. Reassign one with a click and the rule applies to your whole history.

Lives in the tray

Starts with Windows, shows today at a glance and pauses tracking for 15 minutes or an hour. Light or dark, English or Slovak.

Self-hosted. Nothing goes to the cloud.

HomeFocus has no server, no account and no telemetry, and it never connects to the internet. Its only connection is a local one on 127.0.0.1, used by the optional browser extension. Your history is a single SQLite file in your own Windows profile, and you can export or delete it whenever you like.

  • Runs and stores data only on your PC
  • No sign-up, no tracking of you by us
  • Records app names and website domains only: no window titles, no full URLs, no page content, no keystrokes, no screenshots
  • Kept out of roaming profiles, so it never syncs to a server
  • Signed installer, no admin rights needed

Browser extension privacy

The HomeFocus extension exists only to tell the HomeFocus app on the same PC which website you are looking at.

What it reads

The address of the active tab in the focused browser window, from which it keeps only the domain (for example youtube.com). Private windows are never reported.

Where it goes

Only to the HomeFocus app on your own computer, over 127.0.0.1. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party, and nothing is sold or shared.

What it stores

A random pairing token, so the app recognizes the browser you approved. Disconnecting in HomeFocus settings makes it invalid.
