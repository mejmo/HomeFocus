Working from home blurs the day

At the office the day has edges. At home, a quick look at chat becomes half an hour, and the evening slides into "just one more thing". HomeFocus gives you an honest picture of your day, without judgement and without effort: it just runs in the tray.

What you get

Everything you need to see your working day clearly, nothing you have to babysit.

Automatic tracking

Records the app in front of you once a second. Idle time, breaks and sleep are detected and left out.

Day timeline

See your day as a colored strip: work, communication, entertainment and the breaks in between.

Weekly & monthly overview

Totals, daily averages, your busiest day and an app ranking. Export everything to CSV.

Your categories

Apps are sorted automatically. Reassign one with a click and the rule applies to your whole history.

Lives in the tray

Starts with Windows, shows today at a glance and pauses tracking for 15 minutes or an hour.

Looks at home on Windows

Light and dark theme that follows Windows, in English or Slovak.

Self-hosted. Nothing goes to the cloud.

HomeFocus has no server, no account and no telemetry. It does not even contain networking code. Your history is a single SQLite file in your own Windows profile, and you can export or delete it whenever you like.

  • Runs and stores data only on your PC
  • No sign-up, no tracking of you by us
  • Records app names only: no window titles, no keystrokes, no screenshots
  • Kept out of roaming profiles, so it never syncs to a server
  • Signed installer, no admin rights needed
